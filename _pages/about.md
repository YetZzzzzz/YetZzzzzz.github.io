<span class="accent-text">Dr. Yan Zhuang</span> will serve as a Postdoctoral Fellow in the [**Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence**](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/dsai/), <i class="fas fa-university"></i>[**The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU)**](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), Hong Kong SAR, China, advised by Dr. <a href="https://www.polyu.edu.hk/dsai/people/academic-staff/wu-xingyu/">Xingyu Wu</a> and and Prof. <a href="[https://www.polyu.edu.hk/dsai/people/academic-staff/wu-xingyu/](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/dsai/people/academic-staff/tankaychen/)">Kay Chen Tan</a>. He is fortunate to conduct research in the MIND Lab. Before joining PolyU, he received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from <i class="fas fa-university"></i>[**University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)**](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=eyLJ0fMAAAAJ" class="link-accent">Fuji Ren</a>. He received his M.S. in Computer Technology from <i class="fas fa-university"></i>[**UESTC**](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/), under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=6I1ytegAAAAJ" class="link-accent">Yanru Zhang</a>, and his B.S. in Information and Computing Science from <i class="fas fa-university"></i>[**Anhui Science and Technology University**](https://www.ahstu.edu.cn/).

<div class="quote-accent">

His research focuses on self-evolving LLMs and their real-world applications, with broad interests in multimodal intelligence, intelligent agents, and evolutionary approaches for continuous model improvement. His previous research also includes Multimodal Affective Computing and Robust Multimodal Learning.

</div>
